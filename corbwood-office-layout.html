Corbwood — Proposed Office Layout

Based on existing floor plan. Kitchen fixed. All other zones optimised for 1 office + podcast + 6 desks + boardroom + breakout.

Zone Rationale

Kitchen — no change
Stays exactly as-is per your brief. 3.4 × 3.3m at the top-left.
Podcast Studio — existing "Office" room
The existing enclosed room (3.4 × 3.2m) is perfect for a studio — already built out as a private enclosed space, easy to soundproof. No new walls needed. Adjacent to kitchen for breaks between recordings.
Mikey's Office — new glass partition, top-left of main
Create a ~3.5 × 3.5m private office in the top-left of the main open space using a glass partition. Shares the left wall with the podcast studio (keeps the quiet zone on the left side). Glass partition toward the open plan keeps it connected but private.
Breakout — top right, adjacent to kitchen
The 6.5m wide upper-right section (4.6m deep) becomes the breakout lounge. Close to the kitchen so it becomes the natural social zone — people get coffee, sit down, decompress. Informal meeting area doubles the space's utility.
6-Person Desks — central open plan
Main central open space for the team workstations. Good light, central position, natural flow from entrance. Cluster or L-shape arrangement works well in the available footprint.
Boardroom — keep existing
Already purpose-built with the glass wall. 3.6 × 4.9m — seats 8 comfortably. No changes needed except potentially updating fit-out.
Key principle
Quiet zone on the LEFT (podcast studio + Mikey's office) — noise-sensitive activities grouped away from the main floor.

Collaborative zone in the CENTRE (desks, circulation) — team interaction and spontaneous conversation encouraged.

Formal/social zones flanking RIGHT (breakout at top, boardroom at bottom) — natural flow from arrival → boardroom → breakout → kitchen.

Only structural change needed: one glass partition wall for Mikey's office. Everything else works with what's already there.
